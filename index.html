About me

Hi, I’m Alexis.

I’m a sophomore electrical engineering student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, and I’m really into space systems, RF hardware, and anything that has to be built well enough to survive the real world.

My work has gone from lunar mission support and microwave radiometry to cleanroom fabrication and rover avionics. I like projects where the hardware has to be precise, resilient, and actually worth building.

When I’m not in the lab, you’ll probably find me hiking, playing guitar, or looking for the next good view.

HIGP · Student observatory

Ground-based microwave radiometer

I put together a 1.2 m radiometer using an LNB, SDR, Raspberry Pi, and a Python pipeline, then calibrated it enough to measure the Sun at roughly 10,000 K. It’s been one of the most satisfying hands-on builds I’ve done.
