URC Mesh Radio Network & Autonomous Drone

Building a custom drone for the rover team, and the mesh radio network that lets the rover, the drone, and the ground station all talk to each other. Alongside that, early work on autonomy: expanding the rover's camera coverage to train a driving model, and prototyping an LLM-based way to flag anomalies in the drone's imagery.

Building the drone

The airframe is built around a Pixhawk / Orange Cube flight controller. Right now the main constraint I'm working through is power consumption — figuring out what the airframe can actually carry and sustain once the radio, compute, and camera payload are all drawing from the same battery budget.

A mesh network for three nodes

The rover, the drone, and the ground station all need to reach each other, which is a different problem than a single point-to-point link. I'm implementing a mesh radio network so that any node can relay for another — the drone can extend the rover's range back to the ground station, or vice versa, instead of every link depending on a single direct path.

Beyond the radio link

Two threads growing out of this work, both earlier-stage: expanding the rover's camera coverage to gather enough footage to train a locally-run autonomous driving model, and prototyping an LLM-based anomaly detector that looks at the drone's imagery and flags what looks wrong.
